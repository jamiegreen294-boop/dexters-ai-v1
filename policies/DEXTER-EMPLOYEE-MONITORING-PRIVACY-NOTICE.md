# Dexters — Employee Monitoring & Device Privacy Notice

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 21 September 2026

## What Dexters may collect
For Dexters-managed business devices, Dexters may collect device identifier, manufacturer/model, operating-system and app version, device-management status, security/compliance state, installed business-app inventory, last-seen/heartbeat time, device-management job results, and security or fault logs necessary to operate and secure the device.

Dexters does not intend to collect continuous screen contents, keystrokes, microphone recordings, camera images, precise location history, private message contents or personal browsing content as part of normal device management.

## Why we use this information
Purposes include protecting business and customer data; maintaining device security; supporting and troubleshooting business devices; keeping approved software operational; managing company assets; responding to loss, theft, malware and misuse; and demonstrating compliance.

## Lawful basis
Dexters will identify and document the appropriate UK GDPR lawful basis for each processing purpose. For ordinary company-device security and administration, this will commonly be legitimate interests. Where another basis is more appropriate, Dexters will use that basis. Employee consent will not be treated as the default basis for routine workplace monitoring.

## Monitoring principles
Monitoring must have a defined purpose, be necessary and proportionate, use the least intrusive method reasonably available, and be limited to authorised persons. Dexters will not carry out monitoring 'just in case'. Covert monitoring will only be considered in exceptional circumstances with a documented legal and necessity assessment.

## Access and retention
Access to monitoring or management data is restricted to people who need it for an authorised purpose. Data should be retained only for as long as necessary for that purpose, security, legal or audit requirements, then deleted or anonymised.

## Rights and questions
Workers may ask Dexters how their personal information is used and may exercise applicable UK data-protection rights. Concerns should be raised with the business owner/manager responsible for data protection.

## Impact assessments
Before enabling materially more intrusive monitoring (for example continuous location tracking, message-content inspection, screenshots, microphone/call recording or behavioural monitoring), Dexters will complete and document a data-protection impact assessment where required and will update this notice before the monitoring starts.
