# Dexters Business Phone — Monitoring & Device Management DPIA

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 21 September 2026  
**Scope:** Dexter AI management of Dexters-owned Android business phones

## 1. Processing being assessed
Dexter AI manages company-owned phones using Android Device Owner controls and a Dexters device agent. Normal processing may include device identity, model, OS/app version, management state, security/compliance state, installed-app inventory, management commands/results, connectivity/heartbeat status and security/fault logs.

Normal operation does **not** require continuous location tracking, keystroke capture, screenshots, microphone recording, camera capture, private message-content inspection or continuous browsing-history collection.

## 2. Purpose
- protect customer, staff and business information;
- maintain device security and availability;
- support and troubleshoot business devices;
- keep approved applications and configuration working;
- manage company assets;
- respond to loss, theft, malware, compromise or misuse.

## 3. Necessity and proportionality
Dexters will use the least intrusive method reasonably available. Security and health telemetry should be preferred over content monitoring. Access is limited to authorised business administrators. Monitoring must not be enabled merely because the technical capability exists.

## 4. Lawful basis
Dexters will document the appropriate UK GDPR lawful basis for each processing purpose. For ordinary company-device security and administration, legitimate interests is expected to be the principal basis where appropriate. Employee consent is not the default basis for workplace monitoring because of the imbalance in the employment relationship.

## 5. Main privacy risks
- excessive collection of staff personal information;
- accidental capture of personal communications;
- unnecessary location or behavioural tracking;
- unauthorised administrator access;
- excessive retention;
- remote wipe causing loss of personal material;
- automated decisions being made from incomplete or inaccurate device data.

## 6. Controls
- company-owned devices only for full Device Owner management;
- publish the Device Management Policy, Internet/Communications Policy and Monitoring Privacy Notice before routine staff use;
- restrict administration to authorised accounts;
- do not collect content data unless a separate documented necessity assessment supports it;
- no continuous location monitoring by default;
- no covert monitoring except exceptional, documented and lawful circumstances;
- record high-impact actions such as wipe, lock and policy changes;
- use secure authentication for management;
- retain monitoring data only as long as required;
- review the DPIA before adding screenshots, call recording, message-content inspection, location history, biometrics, productivity scoring or automated employment decisions.

## 7. Residual risk
With the above controls, ordinary device-health and security management is intended to be low-intrusion. Any materially more intrusive monitoring requires a fresh DPIA review before activation.
