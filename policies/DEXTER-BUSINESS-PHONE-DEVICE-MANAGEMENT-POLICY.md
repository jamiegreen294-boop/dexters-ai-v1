# Dexters Business Phone — Device Management & Acceptable Use Policy

**Applies to:** Dexters-owned business phones and tablets managed by Dexter AI  
**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom (Scotland)  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 21 September 2026  
**Owner:** Dexters

## 1. Purpose
Dexters provides business devices for legitimate work purposes. Devices may be enrolled in Dexter AI device management so Dexters can secure the device, keep software current, protect business information, support staff, and recover or wipe a lost or stolen device.

## 2. Company ownership and permitted use
The device, its business configuration, business accounts and business data remain the property of Dexters. Reasonable incidental personal use is permitted only where it does not interfere with work, security, costs, customer service or this policy. Staff should not store sensitive personal material on a company device.

## 3. Device management controls
Dexter AI may enforce or manage: screen-lock requirements; operating-system and application updates; approved business applications; device configuration; Wi‑Fi/Bluetooth/security settings; business launcher/home screen; restrictions on adding users, removing management, factory reset, safe boot and unapproved software; device health and compliance checks; remote lock or wipe when a device is lost, stolen, compromised or returned.

Dexters will use the least intrusive control reasonably necessary for the security or operational purpose.

## 4. Security requirements
Users must keep the device physically secure, use the configured screen lock, not share unlock credentials, not bypass Dexter management, not root the device, not disable security controls, not install unapproved software, and report loss, theft, malware, suspicious prompts or unauthorised access promptly.

## 5. Business data
Customer, staff, supplier, payment and other business information must only be accessed for legitimate work. Users must not copy business data to personal accounts, personal cloud storage, removable media or unauthorised apps.

## 6. Remote support and administration
Authorised Dexters administrators may remotely inspect device health, installed app inventory, management status and security/compliance state, and may remotely launch or manage approved apps. Dexters will not use device management for continuous surveillance of staff.

## 7. Lost, stolen or compromised devices
A user must report a lost, stolen or compromised device as soon as possible. Dexters may remotely lock, revoke access, remove business data or wipe the device where proportionate to the risk. Where a wipe is used, Dexters will record the reason and action taken.

## 8. Leaving or changing role
On return, transfer or termination, Dexters may remove access, recover the device, preserve business records where required, and securely erase the device before reassignment or disposal.

## 9. Breaches
Suspected breaches will be investigated fairly. Deliberate circumvention of security or misuse of business data may be handled under the relevant disciplinary process.

## 10. Review
This policy will be reviewed at least annually and whenever material monitoring, device-management or security functionality changes.
