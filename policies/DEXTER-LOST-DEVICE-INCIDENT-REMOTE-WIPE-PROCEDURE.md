# Dexters Business Phone — Lost Device, Security Incident & Remote-Wipe Procedure

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 21 September 2026

1. Record who reported the incident, device, time, circumstances and likely data at risk.
2. Assess immediate risk to customer, staff, supplier, payment and account information.
3. Revoke or rotate affected credentials and sessions where necessary.
4. Use Dexter AI to check last management contact and security state.
5. Lock the device or remove business access where proportionate.
6. Remote-wipe only where necessary to protect information or recover from compromise; record who authorised the wipe and why.
7. Preserve relevant logs without collecting unrelated personal information.
8. Assess whether the incident is a personal-data breach and whether notification to the ICO and/or affected individuals is required.
9. Document containment, recovery and lessons learned.
10. Securely erase and re-provision returned devices before reassignment.
