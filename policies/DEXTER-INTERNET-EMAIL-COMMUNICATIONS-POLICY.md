# Dexters Business Phone — Internet, Email & Communications Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 21 September 2026

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to internet access, web browsing, email, messaging, social media and other communications carried out using a Dexters-owned or Dexter-managed device or business account.

## 2. Acceptable use
Business communications should be professional, lawful and connected to legitimate Dexters work. Limited personal use is acceptable where reasonable, lawful, secure and not disruptive.

## 3. Prohibited use
Users must not use Dexters devices or accounts to: access or distribute illegal content; harass, threaten or discriminate; deliberately access malicious or unsafe sites; bypass security, filtering or management controls; download pirated or unlicensed material; install unapproved apps or browser extensions; disclose customer, staff, supplier or business information without authority; share passwords or authentication codes; or use the device for activity that exposes Dexters to fraud, malware or material reputational risk.

## 4. Internet security
Dexters may use DNS/web protection, safe-browsing controls, malware protection, app restrictions, certificates, VPN or network configuration where proportionate. Security controls may block known malicious, phishing, malware or clearly inappropriate categories of content.

## 5. Monitoring
Dexters may process limited device, app, network and security logs for security, troubleshooting, compliance and investigation of suspected misuse. Monitoring will be proportionate, targeted where possible, and explained in the Employee Monitoring & Device Privacy Notice. Dexters does not authorise routine reading of private communications merely because the technical capability exists.

## 6. Personal accounts
Staff should avoid adding personal email, cloud-storage, social-media or payment accounts to a company phone unless expressly authorised. Where personal use is allowed, staff should understand that a lost/stolen-device response or factory wipe can remove data stored locally on the company device.

## 7. Downloads and software
Only approved apps and files should be downloaded. Unknown-source APK installation is restricted to authorised Dexter administration or an expressly approved support process.

## 8. Reporting
Suspicious links, phishing, malware warnings, unexpected authentication prompts, accidental disclosure or unusual device behaviour must be reported promptly.
