Dexter Business Phone

Fully managed Device Owner provisioning QR for the permanent Dexter Business Phone release.

  1. Only use after confirming the permanent APK installs successfully.
  2. Factory reset the Meizu.
  3. On the first welcome screen, tap the same blank area 6 times to start QR provisioning.
  4. Connect the phone to Wi-Fi when prompted.
  5. Scan this QR.
  6. Allow Dexter Business Phone to finish setup. Do not add a personal Google account during provisioning.
